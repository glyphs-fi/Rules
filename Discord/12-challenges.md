### 7) Challenge rules
In this server, we have some challenge events.
The primary goal of these challenges is to get people's creativity going and to have fun!
Around once every week, a new challenge is posted, and the winners of the previous challenge are voted upon.
To keep things fair and honest, here are some rules about what you can and can't submit for the challenges:
- Be as creative as you want in interpreting the prompt, but keep it recognizable as what it's supposed to be. The moderators have the final say in this matter.
- Submissions should be non-animated image files.
