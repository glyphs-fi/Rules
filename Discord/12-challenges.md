### 7) :GlyphChallengeIcon::AmbigramChallengeIcon::ConlangChallengeIcon:Challenge rules
This server has several (weekly) challenges to sink your creative mind into: 
:GlyphChallengeIcon: **Weekly Glyph Challenge**. 
Every week, we announce a new glyph challenge in #🔠｜glyph-announcements, after which members are encouraged to submit their own typographic interpretations in #glyph-submissions. The purpose of the challenge is to explore the many intresting ways you can express the glyph; whether that be through established font styles, abstract shape language, or artistic interpretations. 
Challenges (roughly) start and end on Thursdays at 11:00 CE(S)T, along with the new glyph announcement, the voting image gets posted & you'll be able to vote on your favourite submissions through the reaction emojis (you can vote on multiple). At the same time, the top 3 submissions of the challenge before will be posted to #🏆｜hall-of-fame and will receive special roles.
-# - Challenges that have `[brackets]` around them are open challenges, and accept any glyph(s) that fit within the category described in the announcement.
-# - Make sure to submit separate submissions as separate images.
-# - Do not submit animated images, as those can't be rendered properly on the voting image.
-# - Don't see your submission in the voting image (properly)? Contact the staff to get it fixed! Usually we replace the image when there's an error.
-# - While it's not a strict rule, submissions should strive to keep the glyph somewhat readable, and to avoid overly illustrative submissions, unless they clearly bend the subject matter to express the glyph. (i.e. avoid stacking random drawings of objects on top of each other to sort of envoke the skeleton of the glyph, but don't hold back on your submission of a dragon or snake beautifully posing as the glyph's stroke flow)
-# - If there's a lowercase/alternative form of a glyph, they are usually allowed unless specified otherwise.
-# - You're allowed to submit multiple times, although different versions of the same submission need to be sufficiently different in letter-form execution.
-# - Make sure to make your submission with :submission: as a reaction emoji! This way our bot will recognise it and include it in our voting images.

:AmbigramChallengeIcons: **Weekly Ambigram Challenge**
Every week, we announce a new ambigram challenge in #🔄｜ambigram-announcements, after which members are encouraged to submit their own ambigrams in #ambigram-submissions. The purpose of the challenge is to explore the craft of making ambigrams, and to encourage unique ways to render a word.
Challenges (roughly) start and end on Thursdays at 11:00 CE(S)T, along with the new ambigram announcement, the voting image gets posted & you'll be able to vote on your favourite submissions through the reaction emojis (you can vote on multiple). At the same time, the top 3 submissions of the challenge before will be posted to #🏆｜hall-of-fame and will receive special roles.
-# - Challenges that have `[brackets]` around them are open challenges, and accept any ambigram(s) that fit within the category described in the announcement.
-# - Make sure to submit separate submissions as separate images.
-# - Do not submit animated images, as those can't be rendered properly on the voting image.
-# - Don't see your submission in the voting image (properly)? Contact the staff to get it fixed! Usually we replace the image when there's an error.
-# - Any and all forms of an ambigram are allowed. Discussions about the validity of an ambigram are welcome.
-# - You're allowed to submit multiple times, although different versions of the same submission need to be sufficiently different in execution.
-# - Make sure to make your submission with :submission: as a reaction emoji! This way our bot will recognise it and include it in our voting images.
